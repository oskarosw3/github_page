Oskar Wachek

Hello, I'm Oskar. I'm currently a student of MSc Digital Humanities at EPFL.

I come from a Computer Science background and I apply the tools of Data Analysis to Social Sciences.
I'm mostly interested about the behaviour of people online and the way they might interact with each other.

Previously I've analysed moderation on Reddit, and now I'm working on the following projects:
- Investigation on how the misinformation spread on Twitter/X is affected by the community structure.
- Inquiry into how the legitimacy of cultural objects is evaluated. Creation of quantified measure of legitimacy, based on video games, movies and music.
